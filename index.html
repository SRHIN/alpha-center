AI for Global Impact

We build culturally grounded, responsible AI systems that improve access to mental health, maternal health and public-health support in underserved communities.

Why we exist

The future of health AI should not require people to speak, live or connect like everyone else.

The AI for Low-Resource Public Health Application Centre is an African hub translating responsible AI research into tools that work in real communities, clinics and health programmes.

We combine public health expertise, lived experience, clinical governance and engineering to build systems that are useful beyond the laboratory.

Our priority

No community left behind by the next generation of healthcare.

Number One

Research that begins with context

Local language, lived experience and health-system constraints shape the problem before a model is selected.

Number Two

Technology designed for access

Low-bandwidth, offline-aware and familiar channels take innovation to the last mile.

Ways AI is changing lives

Mental Health

BraveHeart App

Mental Health Digital Learning Community Empowerment

The BraveHeart App is a digital learning and support platform available on the Google Play Store, designed to extend mental health education and substance use prevention beyond face-to-face sessions.

Mental Health

BraveHeart AI

Youth Nigeria • Sierra Leone Offline First

An AI-powered clinical support tool that helps frontline healthcare workers screen, manage, and refer patients with common mental health conditions using evidence-based guidance.

Explore BraveHeart AI north_east
Generative AI (Pilot)

The GENSCORE AI

Leveraging an “expert-in-the-loop” model and generative AI to co-create a culturally adapted mental health screening tool with psychiatrists, people with lived experience, researchers, linguists, and AI engineers.

Maternal Health

BetaMama Companion

Maternal Neonatal Vaccination

BetaMama Pikin is a transformative initiative committed to reducing maternal and neonatal mortality rates in Nigeria by advancing hygiene practices and promoting life-saving vaccinations during pregnancy, childbirth, and postnatal care.

Maternal Health

MamaWell

Women's Health Emotional Support

A multilingual digital companion that provides Nigerian mothers with free, culturally relevant emotional support and trusted mental health guidance anytime they need it.
